About & Contact

We're a small front-end and full-stack team based in Melbourne, working with technology-driven businesses across Australia.

Our story

NexaTech Solutions started in 2018 when three developers, frustrated with clunky internal tools at their day jobs, decided to build better ones for a living. Today we're a small studio that partners with growing technology, retail, and service businesses to design software people genuinely enjoy using.

We favour clean, maintainable code and honest timelines over flashy promises — and we always start with a working front-end prototype before committing to a full build.
